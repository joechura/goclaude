# StoryBrand (SB7) applied to a website

The visitor is the hero. The organization is the guide. Every section of the homepage maps to one SB7 part. Fill in each slot with the org's real words and facts; mark unknowns `[TODO]`.

## The BrandScript (fill every slot)

| Slot | Question | Website job |
|---|---|---|
| **Hero (audiences)** | Who visits and what do they want? List 2–4 audiences, ranked. | Defines the paths under the primary CTA. |
| **Problem: external** | The tangible obstacle. | Named in the hero subhead or first section. |
| **Problem: internal** | How it makes them feel. | Copy tone; testimonials. |
| **Problem: philosophical** | Why it's just plain wrong. | One bold line; often the best headline material. |
| **Guide: empathy** | "We understand…" | Short line near the founder/story section. |
| **Guide: authority** | Proof: real stats, years, partners, press, results. | Animated numbers, logos, testimonials. |
| **Plan** | 3 simple steps to engage. | A numbered 3-step section. |
| **Direct CTA** | The one main action. | Header button + hero button + repeated at end. |
| **Transitional CTA** | Low commitment (watch, read, subscribe). | Secondary button, never same weight as direct. |
| **Failure (stakes)** | What happens if nothing changes. | One honest sentence, not fear-mongering. |
| **Success** | What life looks like after. | Imagery + closing section. |

**One-liner:** `[Audience] struggle with [problem]. [Org] [plan/what it does] so they can [success].` This becomes the meta description and the "about" summary.

## Multi-audience orgs (nonprofits, marketplaces)
A nonprofit usually serves people who **receive** the mission (e.g. athletes), people who **give time** (volunteers, guides), and people who **give money or reach** (donors, sponsors, partners). Don't pick one and drop the others. The direct CTA should then be a **hub** ("Get Involved") that routes each audience to its path, instead of a bare "Donate" that only speaks to one of them. Donate stays visible as a secondary action in the header/footer.

## Homepage section order (default, adapt as needed)
1. **Hero:** what you do in plain words + direct CTA + transitional CTA + real photo/video
2. **Stakes/problem**: one short line
3. **Guide/authority**: animated real numbers
4. **Plan**: 3 steps / audience paths
5. **Success story**: a real person, real quote, real photo
6. **Gallery / proof**: real media
7. **Final CTA band**: repeat the direct CTA
8. Footer: donate, contact, socials, legal, accessibility statement
